# Artwork provenance

Hand-drawn SVG; no image generation or photographic artwork is used.

Refinement brief: a restrained, grown-up penny loafer in side profile, toe right, with a low throat, shorter vamp and fuller rounded toe, slight toe spring and a separate stacked heel. Use approximately 85–90% of the canvas width. Define the moc-toe apron by an inset tonal plane, not stitching or an outline. Keep a solid strap and a dark slot with a larger blue-dominant rounded inset with three smaller green/yellow/red end pieces. No bulbous toe, bumper sole, large rainbow band, texture or baked highlights/shadows.

Eight SVG layers: heel, sole, upper, lining, apron, strap, slot and penny. Four depth groups preserve overlap; only the apron and strap enable glass. Foreground specular is disabled, with restrained native translucency and shadows. Every source path has a flat opaque fill; no strokes, filters or foreground gradients. Mono uses deliberate grey fills so the silhouette survives Clear and Tinted.

Two directions were drawn and compared at 1024/128/32/16: tan/cream on a native aubergine gradient, and aubergine on a light tile. The saturated gradient was selected at 32 px. The native document is the master; `rebuild.sh` generates the PNG and legacy ICNS from it.

Tower, Amphetamine, Apple's utility icons and Paw's published fox construction informed silhouette, proportion and restraint. No reference artwork was traced or copied. README records the reference study, settings and verification.

Latest refinement preserves the tan-on-aubergine design from `e89d873`: round and fill the forefoot without increasing overall width, move the strap forward to shorten the visible vamp, and retain the heel, palette and native materials. Compare enlarged equal quarters against a blue-dominant penny at 16/32 px; select blue dominance so the smallest render has a clean accent instead of muddy equal colours. At 16 px the four colours need not resolve separately.
